How BLE Pairing Works
  1. Ensure your ESP32 is running the ArkWeather firmware with BLE enabled.
  2. Keep the device within ~10 metres of your computer.
  3. Click "Scan" — your browser will show a popup to select the device.
  4. Once connected, live readings will appear above every 5 seconds.
  5. This is a local viewing mode — data is not sent to the server via BLE. For server push, WiFi is used automatically by the device.
