How BLE Pairing Works
  1. Ensure your ESP32 is running the ArkWeather firmware with BLE enabled.
  2. Keep the device within ~10 metres of your computer.
  3. Click "Scan" — your browser will show a popup listing nearby ArkWeather nodes.
  4. Once connected, live readings update every 2 seconds via BLE notifications.
  5. Optionally select a registered device to also forward readings to the server.
